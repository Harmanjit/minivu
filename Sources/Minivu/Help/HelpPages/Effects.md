# Effects & Retouching

These tools work like the others in the viewer: open them from the
**Image** menu or the left panel, and nothing on disk changes until you
save. See [Editing](help:Editing).

## Color effects

**Image > Effects > Grayscale**, **Sepia** and **Negative** apply at once.
In the left panel they are together under **Color Effects**.

## Effects

- **Drop Shadow…** puts the photo on a larger canvas with a soft shadow.
- **Frame…** adds a border: Solid, Matte, Bevel or Polaroid.
- **Bump Map…**, **Sketch…** and **Oil Painting…** turn the photo into
  embossed, drawn or painted versions of itself.
- **Lens…** magnifies part of the image as if under a lens. Drag the circle
  to move it, or its edge to resize it.

## Text and Shapes

**Image > Text and Shapes…** adds text, lines, arrows, highlights,
rectangles, ovals and callouts. The panel sets colors, line style,
arrowheads, font, alignment and shadow for the selected object.

- Click an object to select it, drag to move it, and use its handles to
  resize or turn it.
- Arrow keys nudge the selection; hold **Shift** to move it further.
  **⌘D** duplicates it, **Delete** removes it and **Esc** deselects it.
- Objects stay editable: open Text and Shapes again straight after, and your
  drawing comes back as objects rather than pixels.

## Clone Stamp and Healing Brush

- **Clone Stamp…** copies one part of the image over another. Option-click
  where to copy from, then paint.
- **Healing Brush…** does the same, but blends the copied texture into its
  surroundings, which is ideal for dust and blemishes. Option-click on clean
  texture, then paint over the spot.
- Set the brush’s size, hardness and opacity in the panel. **[** and **]**
  make the brush smaller and larger.
- **⌘Z** takes back the last stroke while the tool is open.

## Red-Eye Removal

Click **Auto Detect** to find red eyes in faces, or drag a circle over each
red pupil, or click one. Only red pixels inside a circle change, so the
catchlight stays. Drag a circle to move it, set its strength in the panel,
or press **Delete** to remove it.
