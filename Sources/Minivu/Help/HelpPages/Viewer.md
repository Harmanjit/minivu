# Viewer

Double-click a thumbnail or press Return to open it in the viewer. The
viewer opens in a window or in full screen, as set in **Settings > General**;
press Return to switch, and Esc to go back to the browser.

Full screen in minivu is instant, with no animation and no new Space. The
Dock is hidden, and the menu bar appears only when you move the pointer to
the top of the screen.

## Move through the folder

- **→**, **↓** or **Space** shows the next image; **←**, **↑** or **Delete**
  the previous one. **Home** and **End** go to the first and last.
- The viewer steps through the images the browser shows, in its order and
  with its filter. Turn on **Wrap around at end of folder** in Settings to
  go round and round.
- Press **F**, or move the pointer to the top edge, for the filmstrip.

## Zoom and pan

- Click to switch between fitting the window and actual size, centered on
  the point you clicked.
- Drag to pan. When the image is larger than the window, the arrow keys pan
  it instead of changing images.
- Pinch on a trackpad, or use **+** and **−**; **/** shows actual size and
  **\*** fits the window. The **Image** menu has the same commands.
- The mouse wheel shows the next or previous image, or zooms, as chosen in
  **Settings > Viewer**.
- Press and hold to show the magnifier. Its zoom and size are in
  **Settings > Magnifier**.

## Panels at the edges

Move the pointer to an edge of the viewer to reveal a panel:

- **Top:** the filmstrip of the folder.
- **Left:** the editing and effect tools.
- **Right:** file information, camera details and the histogram.
- **Bottom:** zoom, navigation and slideshow controls.

The small overlay in the corner shows the name, position in the folder, size
and zoom whenever they change, then fades. Press **I** to keep it up.

## Stars and tags

Press **0** to **5** to rate the image shown, and **T** or **`** to tag or
untag it. The overlay shows both.

## Documents and animations

- For PDFs and multi-page TIFFs, **Go > Next Page** and **Previous Page**
  (**⌥→** and **⌥←**) move between pages. **Page Down** and **Page Up** go
  through the pages first, then on to the next or previous image.
- Animated GIFs and PNGs play on their own. Press **P** to pause and play.

## Color and HDR

Every image is color managed, so wide-gamut photos look right on every
screen. HDR photos show real highlights on an HDR screen: the Liquid Retina
XDR display of a MacBook Pro, a Pro Display XDR, or an external display with
HDR turned on. Other screens show them tone mapped. You can turn HDR display
off, and choose how RAW files are decoded, in **Settings > Viewer**.

## Two displays

With a second display, the browser can stay on one screen while photos fill
the other:

- Set **Full-screen viewer opens on** in **Settings > Viewer** to
  **Another display** to open the full-screen viewer there.
- **Window > Move to Next Display** moves the window in front to the next
  display.

## Compare

Select two to four images in the browser and choose **Image > Compare
Selected**. The images sit side by side, and zooming or panning one moves
the others with it (the toolbar’s Sync turns that off). Click a pane, or
press **⌘1** to **⌘4**, to choose it: the arrow keys then change its image,
**0** to **5** rate it, **T** tags it and **Delete** moves it to the Trash.

## Formats

minivu opens JPEG, PNG, GIF, HEIC and HEIF, AVIF, WebP, JPEG XL, JPEG 2000,
TIFF, BMP, TGA, ICO, Photoshop (flattened), the camera RAW formats macOS
supports, PDF and SVG.
