# Settings

Choose **minivu > Settings…** (**⌘,**). Every change takes effect straight
away; there is nothing to confirm. **View > Theme** and **View > Live
Text** are settings too, and minivu remembers them.

## General

- **Theme:** System, Bright, Gray or Dark.
- **Show hidden files** in the browser.
- **Wrap around at end of folder:** after the last image, the viewer goes
  back to the first.
- **Open viewer in full screen**, rather than in a window.
- **Ask before saving over the original:** turn this back on if you chose
  not to be asked when saving.
- **Write ratings into files (XMP):** stars, the tag and color labels are
  also written into each photo, so Lightroom, Bridge and other apps see
  them. RAW files, and formats minivu can’t rewrite, get an XMP sidecar
  file beside them instead. Off at first; when you turn it on, only later
  changes are written.

## Viewer

- **Background:** black, dark gray, gray or white behind the image.
- **Enlarge small images to fit** the window or screen.
- **Pixelated zoom above 200%** shows each pixel as a sharp square.
- **Mouse wheel:** previous and next image, or zoom.
- **Full-screen viewer opens on** the display with the browser, or another
  display, so you can browse on one screen and view on the other.
- **Display HDR photos in HDR** on screens that can show it.
- **RAW files:** the embedded preview the camera saved, which is fastest,
  or render the RAW data. minivu renders the RAW data anyway when the
  preview is too small for the screen or the zoom.
- **Render RAW files with extended dynamic range** shows RAW highlights in
  HDR, which takes a moment per photo. **Image > Adjust > RAW
  Adjustments…** follows it too.

## Magnifier

The zoom and size of the loupe that appears when you press and hold on an
image, with a preview.

## Thumbnails

- **Size** of the thumbnails in the grid.
- **Clear Thumbnail Cache** removes saved thumbnails; they are made again
  from the files the next time you look at a folder. Thumbnails minivu has
  already shown stay until you quit and reopen it.

## Slideshow

The time each slide shows, the order, whether to start again after the last
slide, captions (file name, file name and date, or camera and exposure), the
transition and its duration with a preview, and the music: songs or folders,
shuffle and volume. In a show that started with music, changing the volume,
or turning music off or on, applies at once. **Tools > Export Slideshow Video…** uses
the same settings, except the volume.

## Editors

The apps listed in **Tools > Open in External Editor**. Add apps, reorder
them by dragging (the first one is **⌘E**), or pick from suggestions of image
editors installed on your Mac.
