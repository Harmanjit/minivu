# Getting Started

minivu is a fast image browser, viewer and editor for your Mac. It shows a
folder of photos as thumbnails, opens any of them full screen with accurate
color and real HDR, and edits without changing the original until you save.

## Open a folder

- The first time you open minivu it shows your Pictures folder. After that
  it opens the folder you were looking at last.
- Choose **File > Open Folder…** to browse another folder. minivu adds it to
  the sidebar, so it is one click away the next time, even after you quit.
- **File > Add Folder to Sidebar…** adds a folder without leaving the one
  you are in.
- In Finder, open images with minivu (Control-click, then **Open With**),
  or drop a folder on minivu’s icon in the Dock.

> minivu works with folders on your Mac’s internal storage. To look at
> photos on a memory card, a USB drive or a network share, copy them to a
> folder on your Mac first.

## Look at your photos

Double-click a thumbnail, or select it and press Return, to open it in the
[viewer](help:Viewer). The arrow keys and Space move through the folder, and
Esc takes you back to the browser. Return switches the viewer between a
window and full screen.

## Pick the best ones

Press **1** to **5** to give the selected photos stars, or **0** to clear
them. Press the backquote key (**`**) to tag the ones you want to keep. Then
use the Filter menu in the toolbar to show only those. See
[Browser](help:Browser).

## Edit and save

In the viewer, choose a tool from the **Image** menu, or move the pointer to
the left edge of the screen to see them all. Nothing on disk changes until
you choose **File > Save** or **Save As…**. See [Editing](help:Editing).

## Find a command

- Everything minivu does is in the menu bar. To find a command by name,
  type in the search field of the **Help** menu.
- [Keyboard Shortcuts](help:KeyboardShortcuts) lists every shortcut, always
  up to date with the menus.
- minivu never connects to the internet. See [Privacy](help:Privacy).
